## E2: graded query against stored winner sets

Primary verdict: **fail**.

- **(a) GloVe: 0 of 5 k.** Ratio of the fly's AP to the best competitor at
  k = 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 is 0.14, 0.33, 0.46, 0.50, 0.51.
  - The best competitor is the FlyLSH value tag, which stores quantised values
    of the winners.
  - Against Gaussian sign codes, DenseFly and symmetric fly overlap, the fly
    readout's lower bounds are all ≥ 1.19.
- **(b) MNIST: pass.** 1.23 [1.19, 1.26] at k = 2 and 1.10 [1.08, 1.12] at
  k = 4.
- **(c) secondary: fail.** 1 of 4 datasets.

## E3: collision law of the fly hash

Primary verdict: **fail**.

- **(a) Equivalence with dense Gaussian top-k within 5%: 2 of 6 cells.**
  Where the two differ, the sparse fly hash scores *higher*, by 5 to 10% on
  SIFT and GloVe. This was not a registered hypothesis.
- **(b) One-constant half-collision angle.**
  - Held-out random matrices: 19 of 36 cells, where ≥ 90% was required.
  - Connectomes: 7 of 7, all within 1.5°.
  - Both parts were required, so (b) fails.
- **(c) Fan-in boundary: 0 of 4.** At s = d every cell is identical, which
  the criterion did not anticipate.

## E4: derived spectral fan-out rule

Primary verdict: **fail**.

- **Stage 1.** The derived allocation is flat at β = 0, as required. The best
  grid exponent does not decrease in β and k: Spearman over β per k is −0.78,
  −0.95 and +0.63.
- **Stage 2.**
  - The derived allocation lies inside the best grid arm's interval on 0 of 3
    datasets at both k.
  - It beats both even fan-out and validation-chosen input pre-scaling on 2 of
    3 datasets at each k.

## F1: weak-connection audit

B = 1,000 curveball nulls per cell; 7 hemispheres × thresholds of 1, 2, 3 and
5 synapses.

- **All connections.** Q departs from the null in 6 of 7 hemispheres, and
  these 6 remain significant after Holm (adjusted p ≤ 0.010). The hemibrain's
  z is 0.02.
- **At ≥ 2 synapses.** All 7 depart. The hemibrain's z is +2.92,
  p = 0.002.
- The paper's "hemibrain is the exception" statement is revised.

## F2: odour endpoint with B = 2,000 nulls

- All 7 hemispheres are negative, from −3.1% to −0.7%.
- 4 of 7 have unadjusted p < 0.05.
- After Holm, 2 remain: FlyWire R (adjusted p = 0.007) and BANC R
  (adjusted p = 0.018). At B = 100 there were 0.
- The paper now reports these numbers.
