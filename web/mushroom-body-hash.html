A fly brain, hashing odours

Every connection below was measured in a real fruit fly. Pick a smell and it spreads through 1,838 Kenyon cells; the loudest few fire and become the fly's word for that smell. Then scramble the wiring — and watch the word change completely while the answer barely moves.
